How your new system works

From a visitor’s first click to a finished application, in six simple steps.

Three parts, working together

Your websiteBrings in enquiries.
Your contact listKeeps every enquiry and follows up.
Your client portalLets clients follow their case online.

Six steps

  1. Someone visits your website

    They can book a call, take a 1-minute quiz, or download a free guide.

    See it

  2. They leave their details

    Their details go straight into your team’s contact list.

    Your team: adds people who call or message on WhatsApp.See it

  3. They hear back straight away

    They get a thank-you email and can pick a time for a free call. A reminder is sent the day before.

    Your team: keeps their available times up to date.See it

  4. The system keeps in touch

    Helpful emails go out over three weeks. Every lead appears on a board, from “New” to “Won”.

    Your team: writes a short note after each call and moves the lead along.See it

  5. They become a client

    They receive a welcome email and a login to their private client portal.

    Your team: marks the lead as “Won” and opens their case.See it

  6. You work on the case together

    The client sees their progress, documents and messages in one place.

    Your team: ticks off tasks and replies to messages.See it

Your team’s small jobs

  • Every morningCheck new enquiries.
  • After every callWrite a short note.
  • When someone booksStop their emails with one click.
  • Every MondaySend a reminder to people who haven’t booked.
  • When someone signsOpen their case and send a welcome email.
